Abstract
This invented paper describes a porous silicon-graphite battery anode for high-energy cells.

Introduction
Silicon stores lithium through alloying and the composite concept is intended to limit expansion.

Results
The composite anode was assembled in laboratory coin cells. Measured capacity reached 1,420 mAh/g,
and three cells showed similar capacity-fade profiles over repeated cycling.

Conclusion
Future work will evaluate pouch-cell assemblies. The document does not claim a field trial.
